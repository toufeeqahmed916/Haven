Why Haven exists

Getting help shouldn't be another thing to figure out alone.

People often stay silent because they are afraid, unsure who to contact, or don't know how to explain what happened. Haven gives them a clear first step, practical guidance, and a way to follow what happens next.

From personal safety concerns to everyday civic problems, your report should reach the right place without making you navigate a maze of procedures.

Example guidance card

What can I do if I feel unsafe?

If there is immediate danger, move to a safer place if possible and contact the appropriate emergency service. Keep any evidence only if doing so does not put you at risk.

General guidance based on Pakistani law. Not a substitute for a lawyer. Legal references must be verified before use.
Designed with accountability

AI can help organise a report. It cannot make the final call.

Haven may summarize information and suggest an urgency level, but a suggestion is not a verdict. Human reviewers are responsible for confirming urgency and deciding what happens next.
